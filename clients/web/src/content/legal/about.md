# About Blyss

Blyss is a Kenya-focused marketplace where creators can open a storefront, sell digital products and subscriptions, and help buyers find their work.

## For creators

Creators can publish a storefront, list digital products or subscription offers, and manage orders from a dashboard. Buyers can pay using the methods shown at checkout, including M-Pesa and cards where available.

Blyss currently applies a 20% platform fee to completed product sales. The remaining 80% is the creator's share. When a creator has an active Paystack subaccount, Paystack splits the payment to that account; otherwise, the order is recorded for the creator and is not automatically split to a payout account. Paystack processing charges are separate from Blyss's fee and are charged to Blyss's main account on product split payments. Refunds or reversals can change an order's final amounts.

Paystack settles to a creator's linked payout account on its schedule. Its published Kenya schedule is two working days, but settlement timing can vary. Blyss does not promise same-day or 24-hour payouts.

## For buyers

Buyers can browse creator storefronts, compare listings, and purchase digital products or subscriptions. Product formats, access details, price, currency, and available payment methods are shown on the relevant listing and at checkout.

## Our focus

Blyss focuses on practical selling and discovery for creators in Kenya, including local payment options. The catalogue and creator directory show the listings and storefronts currently available on the marketplace.
