# Refunds Policy

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

If a digital product is missing, cannot be accessed, is materially different from its listing, or a payment was duplicated, contact the creator or Blyss support with the order details. Refund requests are reviewed against the listing, payment status, this policy, and any rights provided by applicable law.

## Requesting help

Email **support@blyss.co.ke** from the address used for the purchase. Include your order reference, the product, and a short description of the issue. Do not send full card details or passwords.

We may ask for additional information to understand the request. Refund availability and processing time depend on the issue, payment method, payment provider, and any legal requirements. A payment provider may take additional time to return funds after a refund is approved; Blyss does not promise a fixed refund arrival time.

## Subscriptions

You can manage cancellation from the account or customer portal controls available for your subscription. Cancellation stops future renewals when it takes effect; it does not necessarily refund a period that has already started. Contact support if the cancellation control is unavailable or you believe a renewal was made in error.

## For creators

Approved refunds or payment reversals may change the order and creator-share amounts shown in your finance records. The amount and treatment of payment-provider fees depend on the original transaction and provider response.

Nothing in this policy limits consumer rights that cannot legally be limited. For support, email **support@blyss.co.ke**.
