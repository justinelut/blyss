# Acceptable Use Policy

*Last updated: 26 May 2026*

Blyss is built for independent Kenyan creators selling **digital products and recurring subscriptions** to people who want to support and learn from them. This policy lays out what you can and can't sell on Blyss. Read it carefully — it's binding under our [Terms of Service](/terms).

If something on this list seems ambiguous in your case, email **support@blyss.co.ke** before you list it. We'd rather answer a question than process a takedown.

---

## ✅ What you can sell on Blyss

**Digital downloads:**
- Ebooks, PDFs, study guides, lecture notes
- Templates (Notion, Google Docs, Excel, Figma, design files)
- Code snippets, scripts, plugins, themes
- Photo packs, Lightroom presets, LUTs
- Music beats, sample packs, sound effects, stems
- Video tutorials, animation files, 3D models
- Fonts and lettering (you must own the rights)

**Recurring subscriptions:**
- Paid newsletters
- Members-only communities (Discord access, private feeds)
- Coaching programs delivered remotely
- Monthly content drops (beats-of-the-month, study packs)
- Software-as-a-service licences

**Online services delivered remotely:**
- Recorded courses with video + worksheets
- Group cohorts with structured curricula
- Audio lessons (language, music, prayer)

---

## ❌ What you can't sell on Blyss

**Anything illegal under Kenyan law**, including:
- Weapons, explosives, ammunition
- Drugs (recreational or counterfeit pharmaceutical)
- Stolen, hacked, or pirated content
- Counterfeit branded products

**Adult or sexual content**, including:
- Pornography or sexually explicit material
- Escort services, prostitution
- Anything involving minors in any way

**Hate, harassment, or violence**:
- Content promoting violence against any group
- Hate speech targeting protected characteristics
- Content that doxxes, harasses, or threatens individuals

**Financial fraud and deception**:
- Pyramid schemes, MLMs, "get rich quick" courses without provable results
- Forex/crypto/betting tipping services
- Fake reviews, engagement farming, follower-buying services
- Any scheme that requires recruiting others to make money

**Privacy violations**:
- Hacking tools, exploits, malware
- Stolen personal data, leaked databases
- Tools designed to track or surveil people without consent

**Restricted professional advice**:
- Specific medical, legal, or financial advice presented as professional consultation
  (general educational content is fine — see below)

**Things Blyss doesn't host**:
- Physical goods that need shipping (we're a digital marketplace)
- Live one-on-one services (use a scheduling tool instead)
- Anything you don't have the right to sell

---

## A note on professional advice

You can sell **educational content** about health, law, and finance — for example, a course on personal budgeting, a guide on Kenyan tax filing, or a workbook on stress management. What you can't do is present yourself as someone's doctor, lawyer, or financial advisor through the platform.

Always include a clear disclaimer in your listing if your content touches these areas: *"This is educational content, not personalized [medical / legal / financial] advice. Consult a qualified professional for your specific situation."*

## Enforcement

If we believe a listing or account violates this policy:

1. **First-time minor issues** — we'll email you and give you a chance to fix the listing.
2. **Repeated or clear violations** — we'll remove the listing and may suspend payouts.
3. **Serious violations** (illegal content, fraud, harm) — we'll terminate the account immediately, refund affected Buyers, and report to authorities where required.

We rarely give specific notice for category 3 because it would let bad actors run with the money. If your account is terminated and you believe it was a mistake, email **appeals@blyss.co.ke** with the details.

## Reporting violations

If you see a listing or account that violates this policy, email **report@blyss.co.ke** with:
- A link to the listing or store
- A short description of what's wrong
- Any evidence you have

We review every report and respond within 5 business days.
