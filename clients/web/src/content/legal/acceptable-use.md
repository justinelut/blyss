## Prohibited Content

Creators may **not** sell:

- Malware, exploits, or hacking tools
- Pirated or copyright-infringing content
- Hate speech or content promoting violence
- Sexually explicit content involving minors (zero tolerance)
- Get-rich-quick schemes or fraudulent courses
- Physical goods or drop-shipping
- Anything illegal under Kenyan or international law
