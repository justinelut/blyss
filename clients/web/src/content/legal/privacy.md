## 1. What We Collect

- Email address (for account and transactional communication)
- Name (display on your storefront or orders)
- Phone number (for M-Pesa payments via Paystack)
- Browsing analytics (PostHog, anonymized)
- Device data (Sentry, for error reporting only)

## 2. Payment Data

We do **not** store card numbers or M-Pesa PINs. All payment processing is handled by Paystack. We store only Paystack-issued tokens.

## 3. Why We Collect

- To process purchases and payouts
- To send transactional emails (order confirmation, payout notification)
- To improve the platform (anonymized analytics)

## 4. Third Parties

- **Paystack** — payment processing
- **Resend** — transactional email
- **Cloudflare** — CDN and DDoS protection
- **PostHog** — product analytics (anonymized)
- **Sentry** — error monitoring

## 5. Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time by emailing hello@blyss.co.ke.

## 6. Your Rights (Kenya Data Protection Act 2019)

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact hello@blyss.co.ke.

## 7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for session management. Analytics cookies are anonymized.
