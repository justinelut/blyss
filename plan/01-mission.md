## §0 Mission

Build the public face of **Blyss**: the modern marketplace for Kenyan creators selling digital products and recurring subscriptions.

The website's job — its single, measurable job — is to convert a curious browser into a paying customer in under 90 seconds, and a curious creator into a published storefront in under 10 minutes.

**Brand voice:** confident, Kenyan, modern. We are not Etsy. We are not Jumia. We are not a Shopify theme. The audience is creators tired of Gumroad's 10% fee and PayPal's exclusion of Kenya, and customers tired of shopping experiences that look like they were built in 2014. They will judge Blyss on three axes: payment friction (M-Pesa works first time), page speed (sub-2s LCP on mobile in Nairobi), and visual polish (their phone home screen is full of beautifully-designed apps; we belong there).

**Specialization** (the wedge, not the constraint):

- Digital downloads — templates, ebooks, beats, presets, vector kits, fonts, courses
- Creator subscriptions — recurring tiers with markdown-rendered perks (TikTok exclusives, Drive folders, Notion pages, Discord invites, paid-link bundles)
- Tips and one-off support
- M-Pesa as a first-class payment method

The marketplace catalog is open (anything digital), but the editorial face leads with creator subscriptions and craft digital assets — that's where our visual identity earns its keep.

If a section looks like a Shopify template, rebuild it.
If a screen looks like a SaaS billing dashboard, rebuild it.
The reference is **editorial creator economy**, not e-commerce admin.
