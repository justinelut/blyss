# Kenyan ecommerce taste

When drafting any Blyss UI for Kenyan visitors, anchor in local recognition (Karen / Westlands / Lavington / Kilimani buyers) while holding the editorial-premium bar from `blyss-design`.

## Local references (premium → mainstream)
- **adeledejak.com** — Kenyan luxury accessories; the strongest local editorial-commerce reference (premium African brand voice).
- **untitledafrica.com** — African editorial commerce, strong type.
- **soko.co.ke**, **kissamboo.com** — Kenyan handcraft/beauty with a genuine creator narrative.
- **vivowoman.com**, **lapaire.com**, **shopzetu.com** — mainstream/mid-market Kenyan e-commerce; payment-method-aware, modern.

## Anti-references (never emulate)
- **jumia.co.ke** — busy, badge-heavy, low-trust. The local AI-slop trap.
- **kilimall.co.ke**, **masoko.com** — generic Bootstrap / outdated commerce.

## Tone
- Modern, confident, neutral. Not Etsy-cute, not Jumia-shouty. "Kenyan craft, internet-grade polish."
- No slang in UI copy ("Mzee", "Bro", "Sasa"). No tourist clichés (no safari/Maasai/"Hakuna Matata" motifs).
- The work is the proof — no trust badges, no "as seen on", no star ratings.

## Exact formatting conventions
- **M-Pesa** — exact casing. Never "Mpesa", "MPesa", "M-PESA", "mpesa".
- **Money** — prefix **KSh** with a space: `KSh 1,500`. Not "Ksh", "KES", "Kshs", "/=". USD only as the secondary currency (KES + USD only in v1).
- **Phone** — `+254 7XX XXX XXX` with non-breaking spaces; never collect or display M-Pesa PINs.
- **Address** — "Nairobi, Kenya" or just "Nairobi". Never "Nairobi, KE".
- **Payout promise** — "Paid out within 24 hours" (M-Pesa or card via Paystack).
- Lead the editorial face with creator subscriptions + craft digital assets; M-Pesa-first payment CTA on product pages.
